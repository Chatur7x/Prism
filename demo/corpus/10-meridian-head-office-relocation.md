# Meridian Group — head office relocation

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Facilities
**Date:** 2 April

## 1. Relocation

The head office moved during the Easter closure. The move was completed ahead of
schedule and the old site was released on the same day.

Meridian Group headquartered_in Corvale.

## 2. Consequences

Two records were affected by the move: the corporate register entry and the
insurance schedule. Both have been updated, but the register update was applied
after the insurance schedule, which is why the two records disagreed for eleven
days.

## 3. Note on earlier statements

The January briefing named a different principal place of business. That
statement is out of date and is superseded by this notice.
