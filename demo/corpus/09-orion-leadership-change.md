# Orion Systems — leadership change notice

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Company Secretary
**Date:** 22 March

## 1. Appointment

The board of Orion Systems appointed a new chief executive with effect from the
start of April. The notice was circulated to group finance on the same day.

Orion Systems chief_executive Marcus Reyes.

## 2. Continuity

No other executive changes were made. The platform lead and the commercial
director continue in post, and the migration programme ownership question
remains open.

## 3. Record-keeping

This notice is the authoritative record of the appointment. Where earlier
internal correspondence named a different successor, that correspondence is
superseded and should not be relied on.
