# Aster Labs — reporting line correction

**Classification:** Restricted
**Author:** Research Office
**Date:** 9 April

## 1. Correction

The research office previously recorded the reporting line for Aster Labs
against the wrong entity. The group secretariat confirmed the correct line in
April and asked that the earlier briefing be withdrawn from circulation.

Aster Labs reports_to Northstar Holdings.

## 2. Effect of the correction

The February briefing remains valid in every respect except the reporting line.
The funding arrangement described there is unchanged.

Northstar Holdings funds Aster Labs.

## 3. Outstanding

The casting-authority question raised in February remains unresolved. The group
audit committee has scheduled it for the May session.
