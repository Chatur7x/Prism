# Pinnacle Freight — logistics agreement summary

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Logistics
**Date:** 15 March

## 1. Counterparties

Pinnacle Freight provides haulage to three group companies under standing
agreements. The agreements were renewed in the first quarter on unchanged terms.

Pinnacle Freight supplies Orion Systems.
Pinnacle Freight supplies Calder Dynamics.
Pinnacle Freight supplies Northstar Holdings.

## 2. Management

The executive team changed in March. The appointment was announced internally
before any external disclosure.

Pinnacle Freight chief_executive Dana Whitfield.
Pinnacle Freight headquartered_in Corvale.

## 3. Service levels

Service levels were met in every month of the quarter under review. Two
instances of late delivery were recorded against the Halden route and were
attributed to weather rather than to capacity.
