# Facilities note — deliberately uninformative

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Facilities
**Date:** 9 July

## 1. General

This note covers building maintenance scheduling for the second half of the
year. The works programme was agreed with the landlord and no structural work is
required before the winter inspection.

Air handling units were serviced in June. Filter changes are on the standard
cycle and no exceptions were recorded. The fire alarm system was tested in May
and passed without a recorded defect.

## 2. Access

Contractor access to the roof is restricted to booked slots. Two contractors
requested unscheduled access for survey work; both were redirected to the
standard booking process rather than being granted an exception.

## 3. Unrelated matters

The parking allocation working group did not meet in July because the quorum was
not reached. The item was deferred to August. The bicycle store repair has
remained outstanding since May and the working group has asked facilities to
escalate it to the site manager.

## 4. Note

Nothing in this note concerns the corporate structure, ownership, or reporting
lines of any group entity. It is filed with the demo corpus so that retrieval
has realistic non-answering material to reject, rather than only text that
happens to match.
