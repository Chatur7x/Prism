# Northstar Holdings — risk register extract

**Classification:** Restricted
**Author:** Group Risk
**Date:** 8 May

## 1. Concentration risk

Supplier concentration remains the highest-scored operational risk. Four
suppliers serve five group sites, and the group has no qualified alternative for
two of them.

Pinnacle Freight supplies Orion Systems.
Pinnacle Freight supplies Calder Dynamics.
Vantage Materials supplies Calder Dynamics.
Aster Labs supplies Calder Dynamics.

## 2. Governance risk

Two governance questions are open: casting authority over the research budget,
and ownership of the shared services migration programme. Neither has an owner
with a deadline.

## 3. Review cadence

The risk register is reviewed quarterly by the audit committee. The next review
is scheduled for the August session.
