# Group secretariat — reconciliation log extract

**Classification:** Restricted
**Author:** Group Secretariat
**Date:** 25 June

## 1. Purpose

This log records every known divergence between internal briefings and the
corporate filings. It exists because operational briefings are written by
functions that do not have access to the filings.

## 2. Open items at the time of writing

Four divergences were open when this extract was taken. Each names the filings as
the authoritative record.

Meridian Group headquartered_in Corvale.
Aster Labs reports_to Northstar Holdings.
Vantage Materials subsidiary_of Northstar Holdings.
Orion Systems chief_executive Marcus Reyes.

## 3. Closed items

The principal place of business for Meridian Group was reconciled in April. The
operating entity for Calder Dynamics was determined in April. Both items are
closed.

## 4. Method

Divergences are logged when a briefing is found to disagree with the filings,
not when the briefings disagree with each other. Two briefings can both be
internally consistent and still both be wrong.
