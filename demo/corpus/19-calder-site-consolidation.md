# Calder Dynamics — site consolidation plan

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Facilities
**Date:** 4 June

## 1. Plan

Facilities proposes closing the Corvale site and consolidating operations at
Halden. The proposal is at concept stage and has not been approved.

## 2. Supplier implications

Consolidation would reduce the volume available to two suppliers. Procurement
has been asked to model the effect before any decision is taken.

No decision has been taken. This document is a proposal and carries no
