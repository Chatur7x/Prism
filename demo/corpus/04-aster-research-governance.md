# Aster Labs — research governance briefing

**Classification:** Restricted
**Author:** Research Office
**Date:** 27 February

## 1. Reporting line

Aster Labs reports through the group rather than directly to the holding
company, following a clarification issued by the group secretariat in
February. Earlier correspondence described a different line, which this briefing
supersedes.

Aster Labs reports_to Orion Systems.
Aster Labs parent_organization Orion Systems.

## 2. Funding

Research funding for the coming year was agreed at the February session. The
funding arrangement is a grant rather than an equity purchase, which changes the
consolidation treatment.

Meridian Group funds Aster Labs.
Orion Systems supplies Aster Labs.

## 3. Governance gap

The briefing records an unresolved question about who holds casting authority
over the research budget. The question is referred to the group audit committee
and is not settled by this document.
