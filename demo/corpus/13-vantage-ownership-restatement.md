# Vantage Materials — ownership restatement

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Group Finance
**Date:** 25 April

## 1. Restated position

Group finance has restated the ownership of Vantage Materials following the
reconciliation of the register against the corporate filings. The restated
position is now the single authoritative record.

Vantage Materials parent_organization Northstar Holdings.
Vantage Materials subsidiary_of Northstar Holdings.

## 2. What changed

The January register and the February accounts named an intermediate holding
company. Both were wrong. The filings show that Vantage Materials has been a
direct subsidiary of the holding company since the acquisition.

Northstar Holdings controls Vantage Materials.

## 3. Renewals

The fourth-quarter renewals will be priced on the current basis pending a
written position from group finance. No renewal has been signed.
