# Aster Labs — budget authority question

**Classification:** Restricted
**Author:** Research Office
**Date:** 28 May

## 1. Status

The casting-authority question raised in February was considered by the group
audit committee in May. The committee did not reach a conclusion and asked the
group secretariat to bring a written options paper to the June session.

Northstar Holdings funds Aster Labs.
Meridian Group funds Aster Labs.

## 2. Interim arrangement

Until the question is settled, the existing arrangement continues. Both funders
have confirmed that no budget is affected by the delay.

Aster Labs parent_organization Northstar Holdings.

## 3. Why this document exists

This document exists to record that the question is open. It does not resolve
the question, and it should not be cited as if it did.
