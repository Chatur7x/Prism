# Meridian Group — group accounting policy

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Group Finance
**Date:** 30 April

## 1. Consolidation

Group companies are consolidated where the group holds a controlling interest,
irrespective of the reporting line described in operational briefings. The
reporting line and the consolidation treatment are distinct questions and are
frequently confused in internal correspondence.

Meridian Group parent_organization Northstar Holdings.
Meridian Group invests_in Aster Labs.
Meridian Group invests_in Helix Consortium.

## 2. Related-party transactions

All related-party transactions are disclosed in the annual accounts. The
quarterly register is a control document and is not a substitute for the
statutory disclosure.

## 3. Known divergences

Two internal records are known to disagree with the filings at the time of
writing: the reporting line for one research subsidiary, and the principal place
of business for this entity. Both divergences are tracked in the group
secretariat's reconciliation log.
