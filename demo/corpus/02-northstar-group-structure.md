# Northstar Holdings — group structure note

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Group Finance
**Date:** 4 February

## 1. Control positions

The group holds a controlling interest in two operating companies. Neither
position is contested and no minority buy-out is planned this financial year.

Northstar Holdings controls Orion Systems.
Northstar Holdings controls Vantage Materials.
Northstar Holdings controls Meridian Group.

## 2. Governance

The board of Northstar Holdings is unchanged since the last annual meeting.
Board papers are circulated through the group secretariat rather than directly
to subsidiary management, which shortens the notice period for ad-hoc items.

Northstar Holdings headquartered_in Corvale.

## 3. Related parties

Transactions with Orion Systems continue to be conducted on arm's-length terms.
The related-party register was reviewed in January and no exceptions were noted.
