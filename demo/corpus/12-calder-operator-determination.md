# Calder Dynamics — operator determination

**Classification:** Internal
**Author:** Facilities
**Date:** 18 April

## 1. Determination

Following the reconciliation of the site register, the group concluded that
Calder Dynamics is operated by the holding company rather than by the
intermediate holding company. The determination supersedes the March register.

Calder Dynamics operated_by Northstar Holdings.

## 2. Supplier consolidation

Procurement has confirmed the consolidated supplier base. The four suppliers
recorded in March remain in place and no further consolidation was approved.

Calder Dynamics sources_from Vantage Materials.
Calder Dynamics sources_from Pinnacle Freight.
Calder Dynamics sources_from Aster Labs.
Calder Dynamics sources_from Orion Systems.

April and filed with the audit committee. The committee has not yet responded.
