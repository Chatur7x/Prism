# Ardent Logistics plc — supply and distribution review

**Classification:** Internal — Group Commercial
**Date:** 2 April

## 1. Scope

This paper covers the group's outbound distribution arrangements as at April. It
does not cover inbound transport, which is reported separately by the Cardinal
Freight Group operations director, and it does not attempt a valuation.

## 2. Outbound arrangements

Ardent Logistics supplies three counterparties under standing schedules: Halcyon
Cold Stores, the Brackwell Retail Group, and a consortium trading as Vellmark
Distribution. Each schedule is reviewed annually.

Halcyon Cold Stores is supplied from the Corvale depot. The Brackwell Retail Group
is supplied from Drayton. Vellmark Distribution is supplied from Halden, although
the volumes are small enough that the group does not regard the relationship as
material.

## 3. Related positions

The group also holds reciprocal positions that are frequently mistaken for supply
arrangements. Halcyon Cold Stores supplies frozen capacity back to the group under
a separate agreement, and the distinction matters for the segment disclosure.

Ardent Logistics collaborates with the Kestrel Engineering partnership on
warehouse automation, and Ardent Logistics invests in Kestrel Engineering through a
minority stake held by the group's innovation vehicle.

## 4. Deliberate exclusions

Several counterparties named in earlier drafts were removed from this paper because
the relationship is not held at group level. Meridian Group was mentioned at the
February meeting but no group entity has a contractual relationship with it, and
the paper is clear that none exists.

Norwick Haulage was similarly discussed and removed. Nothing in this paper should be
read as implying that the group holds any position in Norwick Haulage.
