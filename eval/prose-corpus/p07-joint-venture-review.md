# Joint venture review — Kestrel automation programme

**Classification:** Internal — Group Development
**Date:** 24 June

## 1. Programme history

The Kestrel Engineering automation programme began in 2021 under a collaboration
agreement between the Ardent Logistics plc group and Kestrel Engineering. That
agreement was succeeded by a joint-venture agreement in 2023, under which the
programme is now run.

## 2. Governance

Under the joint-venture agreement neither party controls the programme. The
Ardent Logistics plc group funds the programme and Kestrel Engineering operates it.
Decisions of substance require agreement, which has not always been forthcoming.

The programme is therefore not owned by the group and should not be described as a
group subsidiary.

## 3. Financial

The group has committed a further tranche to the programme. The commitment is
conditional and has not been drawn. The group's minority stake in Kestrel
Engineering was reduced in 2024; the group no longer holds an equity position of
any size.

## 4. Relationship to the foundation

The Ardent Foundation is not a party to the joint-venture agreement. The foundation
administers the Halden University fellowship and is wholly separate from both the
group and the programme.
