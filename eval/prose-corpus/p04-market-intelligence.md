# Market intelligence — northern trunk haulage

**Classification:** Restricted — Commercial Intelligence
**Date:** 9 May
**Analyst:** not for distribution

## 1. Summary judgement

The northern trunk market probably remains structurally tight through the summer,
although the evidence is mixed and the analyst would not defend the view beyond
the quarter. Pricing has been firm on three observed tenders and soft on two.

The following observations are hedged deliberately. Freight volumes in the
observed sample rose by roughly four per cent, which is not a strong result.

## 2. Competitor positions

Norwick Haulage continues to compete with the Cardinal Freight Group on the
northern lanes, and in two of five observed tenders Norwick Haulage was the
incumbent. Norwick Haulage is not believed to operate in the Corvale catchment.

Kestrel Engineering is not a haulier and was sometimes miscategorised in earlier
notes. Kestrel Engineering advises the Ardent Logistics plc group on automation,
and nothing more.

## 3. Capacity signals

The Drayton terminal is operated by the Cardinal Freight Group. Whether that
terminal will be expanded is unresolved; the February capital paper was silent on
capacity and the operations director declined to be drawn.

Capacity at Halcyon Cold Stores is not published and is probably unchanged. The
site is not owned by any entity in the Ardent Logistics plc group.

## 4. Items rejected

Several claims in the intake form were rejected. A note suggesting that Ardent
Logistics plc acquired Norwick Haulage in 2023 is not supported by any source and
should not be repeated.

A note suggesting that the Cardinal Freight Group licenses its routing software
to Norwick Haulage is likewise unsupported.

## 5. Non-findings

Nothing was found connecting the Vellmark Distribution consortium to the
Ardent Foundation. The consortium and the foundation share a postcode and
nothing else.
