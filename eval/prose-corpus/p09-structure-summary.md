# Corporate structure summary — consolidated

**Classification:** Internal — Group Finance
**Date:** 2 August

## 1. Basis of preparation

This summary is prepared for the consolidated accounts and states group structure
as at the date. It supersedes all earlier structure notes.

## 2. Group entities

The Ardent Logistics plc parent company owns the Halden distribution centre and
controls the Cardinal Freight Group operating company. The Cardinal Freight Group
is a subsidiary of the Ardent Logistics plc parent.

Cardinal Freight Group is succeeded by no entity; it remains in operation.

## 3. Entities outside the group

Kestrel Engineering is operated jointly with the Ardent Logistics plc group under
a joint-venture agreement. Kestrel Engineering is not a subsidiary of the group.

Halcyon Cold Stores is independent. The Ardent Logistics plc group supplies
Halcyon Cold Stores and does not control it.

The Ardent Foundation is independent and is not part of the group.

Vellmark Distribution is a customer and is not part of the group.

## 4. Contradictions with earlier documents

Two earlier documents are inconsistent with this summary and are withdrawn.

The November structure note stated that the Cardinal Freight Group reports_to the
Ardent Logistics plc parent. That statement is wrong. The Cardinal Freight Group
reports_to the group finance committee, not to the parent entity, and the
distinction is material for governance purposes.

The November structure note also stated that Halden is the group's registered
office and that the Halden distribution centre is therefore a group site. The
registered office and the distribution centre are separate. The distribution
centre is at Hollowfield Way; the registered office is on Vellmark Row.
