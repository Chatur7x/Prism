# Site register — physical and operating locations

**Classification:** Internal — Estates
**Date:** 17 May

## 1. Purpose

This register records where group sites are and who operates them. Operating
responsibility and ownership are recorded separately because they frequently differ.

## 2. Sites operated by the group

The Corvale terminal is operated by the Cardinal Freight Group under a lease
running to 2031. The site is not owned by the group.

The Halden distribution centre was built by the group in 2014 and is owned by the
Ardent Logistics plc parent company. It is not administered by the Cardinal
Freight Group, although Cardinal Freight Group staff have day-to-day charge of
loading.

The Drayton yard is leased from a local authority and is operated by the Cardinal
Freight Group.

## 3. Sites the group does not operate

Brackwell Cold Store is owned by Halcyon Cold Stores and is operated by that
company. The Ardent Logistics plc group supplies into the site but has no operating
responsibility for it.

Vellmark Distribution operates from a unit at the same address as the Ardent
Logistics plc registered office. Co-location is not ownership and the two
entities are not related.

## 4. Historical entries

The Hollowfield site was closed in 2019. The site was succeeded by the Halden
distribution centre, which opened in 2014. The chronology is deliberately
unusual and the register records it as found.
