# Personnel briefing — senior appointments

**Classification:** Internal — Group Secretariat
**Date:** 28 April

## 1. Purpose

This briefing records senior appointments and departures for the quarter. It is
written for the board and is not a general announcement.

## 2. Appointments

Sunniva Halden-Reid was appointed chief executive of the Ardent Logistics plc
operating company with effect from 1 May. She succeeds Ivor Marchetti, who held
the post for six years.

Miss Halden-Reid was, until January, the operations director of the Cardinal
Freight Group. She therefore reports to a group she previously ran, a point the
board Chair raised and which the nomination committee considered manageable.

## 3. Departures

Two departures were recorded. Tomas Bramwell left the Halden site in March; his
function has not been recruited. Adaeze Okonkwo remains in post and is not
affected by the reorganisation.

## 4. Corrections to earlier briefings

The February briefing stated that Ivor Marchetti would retire at the end of the
year. That statement was wrong. Mr Marchetti's departure was a matter of board
policy and the date was never fixed.

The February briefing also stated that the group funds a fellowship at Halden
University. It does not. The fellowship is administered by the Ardent Foundation,
which is a separate entity and is not controlled by the group.
