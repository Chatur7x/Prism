# Regulatory and legal summary

**Classification:** Legal privilege asserted
**Date:** 3 June

## 1. Matter summary

This note summarises the Kestrel Engineering dispute and the related
counterclaims. It is privileged and is not to be distributed outside Legal.

## 2. The claim

The Ardent Logistics plc group is joined as a party to proceedings brought by
Halcyon Cold Stores. The claim concerns capacity charges at the Brackwell site and
does not, on the evidence currently available, concern any ownership question.

## 3. Response

It is not admitted that the group owes the sums claimed. The group maintains that
no sum is due and that the claim is misconceived.

The Ardent Logistics plc group is separately regulated by the Halden Transport
Authority, and the group is not advised that any regulatory finding has been made
against it.
