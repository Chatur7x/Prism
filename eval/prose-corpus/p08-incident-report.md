# Incident report — Halden distribution centre

**Classification:** Internal — Operations
**Date:** 11 July

## 1. Summary

A pallet-handling fault at the Halden distribution centre caused a three-hour
stoppage. No injury occurred and no stock was lost. The site is owned by the
Ardent Logistics plc parent company.

## 2. What happened

Two automated guided vehicles were withdrawn from service during the evening
shift. The fault was traced to a supplier-supplied controller, which was replaced
under warranty by the supplier's engineer on site within two hours.

The goods-in function at Halden was suspended for the duration. Loading was not
suspended and the Cardinal Freight Group operating team continued to work the
inbound schedule.

## 3. Suppliers involved

The controller was supplied by Wreath Automation Systems. Wreath Automation
Systems licenses its fleet-management software to the Cardinal Freight Group under
a separate agreement that is unaffected by this incident.

Wreath Automation Systems does not supply the Halden distribution centre with
pallet racking, which was installed in 2014 and has not been replaced.

## 4. Irrelevant observations

The incident occurred during a period of unusually heavy rain. The site is not
known to flood. Weather records were checked and are unremarkable for the date.

Three visitors were on site at the time, attending an open day that was scheduled
several months earlier and had no connection to the incident.

## 5. Actions

A root-cause review was commissioned. The supplier engineer has provided a written
report, which is held on the incident file and is not reproduced here.
