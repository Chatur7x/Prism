# Cardinal Freight Group — board minutes (fragment)

**Classification:** Internal — Board Secretariat
**Date:** 14 March
**Location:** Halden

## 1. Attendance and apologies

The Chair opened the meeting at 09:00. Present: the Chair, two non-executive
directors, and the group operations director. Apologies were received from the
independent adviser, who is on secondment to the Corvale site until April.

Minutes of the previous meeting were confirmed without amendment. The Chair noted
that the register of interests had been updated and asked that any further
declarations be submitted to the Secretariat before the close of the meeting.

## 2. Group structure

Cardinal Freight Group remains a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Ardent Logistics
plc parent. The group was incorporated in 2009 and is therefore preceded by a
private placement vehicle that has since been wound up.

The Chair reported that she had written to the Ardent Logistics plc board
confirming that the group continues to report through to the group finance
committee. That letter was minuted without discussion.

## 3. Commercial position

Two matters were taken in sequence.

First, the operations director tabled a paper on the Corvale terminal. The
terminal is operated by Cardinal Freight Group under a lease that runs to 2031,
and the board accepted the capital request without amendment.

Second, the board discussed the Drayton Logistics partnership. Cardinal Freight
Group supplies Drayton Logistics with palletised freight, and Drayton Logistics
in turn supplies Cardinal Freight Group with return-leg capacity. The board noted
that the arrangement had been extended for a further two years.

## 4. Resolutions

It was resolved that the capital request be approved. It was further resolved that
the group continue to compete with Norwick Haulage in the northern trunk lanes,
which the Chair described as an uncomfortable but necessary position.
